Custom Printed Boxes

Explore custom boxes that are stylish and functional, perfectly crafted to showcase, store, and protect your products.

Mailer Boxes

Offer secure, stylish packaging for e-commerce and subscription products. Designed for durability and brand impact, they enhance presentation and protect contents during transit.

Tuck Boxes

Compact and sturdy, small tuck boxes are ideal for packaging lightweight items like cosmetics, jewelry, or gifts. Their easy-to-close tuck flap ensures secure storage with a neat, professional look.

Rigid Boxes

Premium, sturdy packaging solutions ideal for luxury products. With a high-end finish and solid structure, they deliver an exceptional unboxing experience and strong brand presence.

Dispenser Boxes

Custom dispenser boxes are specially designed cardboard packages that provide convenient, controlled access to their contents through easy-to-tear perforated openings, often called "flip-out dispenser" openings.

Cigarette Boxes

Stylish and protective, cigarette boxes are designed to preserve freshness and enhance brand appeal with customizable finishes, sizes, and secure closures.

Burger Boxes

Designed to keep your burgers fresh, secure, and visually appealing. Perfect for dine-in, takeout, or delivery, they offer durability, insulation, and custom branding options.

Magnetic Closure

Premium unboxing experience with their sleek design and secure magnetic flap. Ideal for luxury products and gifts, they combine elegance with functionality.

Cosmetic Boxes

Enhance your brand’s image while securely packaging skincare and beauty products. Sleek, durable, and fully customizable, they’re perfect for both retail display and gifting.

How it works

Creating custom boxes is easy with us! You can get boxes delivered to your doorstep in just four simple steps.

1. Consultation

When you share the details, we will recommend the best material and box style to create a perfect box for your product.

2. Design Preparation

After choosing the style and material, we start creating your box design. If you already have one, we can check it for errors.

3. Production

Once the design and price are finalized, we will forward the job to the printing press, and they will start producing your boxes.

4. Delivery

When production is completed, we double-check everything and pack your boxes in a shipping box for safe delivery.

FAQs on Custom Boxes

We've answered all the common questions you may have before ordering a custom box.

Can I get a sample before placing an order? ▼

Yes, you can get a sample before placing an order, but it is available only on demand, and you will have to pay for it.

Can I provide my own artwork? ▼

Yes, you can provide your own artwork, but you will need to get in touch with our designers for this. They will guide you regarding the format and resolution requirements.

How do I know if my design is ready to print? ▼

Our design team will review your design for errors like artwork resolution or blurriness, etc. If they find any issues in your design, they will inform you within the proof and help in resolving them. Please note that our team will not review grammar and spelling mistakes.

Can I print inside the box? ▼

Yes, you can print your design on the outside, inside, or both sides of your product boxes. Prices change if you want to print on both sides.

Shall I pay for plates or die charges? ▼

No, you won't need to pay any extra amount for plates or dies, even for shipping. There are no hidden charges; the prices you see on the quote are final.

What factors affect the pricing? ▼

Generally, five things affect the price: box material, dimensions, box style, ink usage on the box, and quantity. If you order in bulk, you will get custom boxes at wholesale prices. If you need further information regarding prices, you can contact us anytime, and we will assist you.

When will I receive my custom product boxes? ▼

It takes 8 to 10 business days to produce your personalized boxes, and an additional 3 to 4 days for delivery. Rush orders can be completed in 4 to 5 business days.

Can you ship outside the United States? ▼

Yes, it is possible to deliver boxes to countries other than the USA. First, you will need to make sure that your box meets the size and weight restrictions of the shipping company.

Are there any hidden charges? ▼

No, there are no hidden charges for custom packaging solutions. The prices you see in the final quotation are the ones you will pay.

How do I choose the right packaging material for my product? ▼

Our team of experts can assist you in selecting the most suitable packaging material based on your product’s size, weight, and protection needs.
